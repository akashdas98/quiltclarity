## Selection assumptions

- Global English-speaking audience.
- The purchased domain determines the final public brand.
- Prefer an ordinarily priced `.com`.
- No hyphens, numbers, awkward misspellings, or expensive aftermarket acquisition.
- The brand must cover exact stock and purchase reconciliation, the cutting planner,
  standalone calculators, and Guides.
- The brand must not imply a full quilt-design application.

## Research method and limitations

The 2026-08-10 review used:

- live Verisign `.com` RDAP checks for registration status;
- exact-name and adjacent-product web searches;
- current competitor evidence already recorded in the launch package;
- Internet Archive CDX checks for prior captures of the finalists; and
- current official registrar pricing/features pages.

`NOT_FOUND` from the `.com` registry means that no current registration record was
returned at the time of the check. It is not a reservation or purchase guarantee.

Exact-name web and archive searches are common-law/confusion screening inputs, not
trademark clearance. WIPO explicitly prohibits automated querying of its Global
Brand Database, and the current USPTO and IP India search systems require
interactive use. Those official searches remain part of the final purchase gate.

### 1. QuiltClarity

Strengths:

- expresses the product's strongest positioning: understandable, inspectable
  quilting calculations;
- works for the planner, calculators, guides, and methodology content;
- easy to spell and pronounce;
- does not imply a full design canvas; and
- exact-name searches found no prominent incumbent, while the archive index
  returned no captures for the domain.

Tradeoff:

- the name alone does not say “calculator” or “cutting planner,” so homepage and
  search copy must continue explaining the utility immediately.

### 2. QuiltCutPlan

Strengths:

- describes the centerpiece workflow directly;
- distinguishes the product from general quilt-design tools; and
- exact-name searches found no prominent incumbent or archive captures.

Tradeoffs:

- reads as three compressed words;
- feels more mechanical; and
- gives the standalone non-cutting calculators less room than QuiltClarity.

### 3. QuiltPilot

Strengths:

- short, pronounceable, brandable, and broad;
- can cover future evidence-driven extensions without implying a designer; and
- exact-name searches found no prominent incumbent or archive captures.

Tradeoff:

- does not communicate measurement, cutting, or yardage without supporting copy.

### 4. QuiltYardage

Strengths:

- immediately communicates a high-value search task;
- familiar language for the intended audience; and
- the `.com` registry returned no record.

Tradeoffs:

- highly descriptive and therefore less distinctive;
- suggests yardage calculation more than cutting-plan generation; and
- constrains the broader calculator family more than QuiltClarity.
