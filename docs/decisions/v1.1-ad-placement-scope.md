# Owner-Locked Ad Placement Scope

## Decision

Owner instruction, 2026-10-06, governs placement design:

- Desktop side rails are the default across page templates, including homepage,
  directory, guide, calculator, planner and informational pages. Earlier blanket
  homepage/informational exclusions are superseded. Rails use available margins
  without narrowing or overlapping the existing content. Both sides and multiple
  vertically spaced units are in scope; no one-unit-per-side or per-page cap is
  agreed. Actual count follows each page's length, spare geometry and provider
  constraints; short pages must not acquire artificial length to hold ads.
- Planner inline placements are locked after Project, after Fabrics, after Cut
  Requirements and its Calculate button, and after the complete final result.
  They apply on mobile and desktop. Rails supplement these inline placements on
  sufficiently wide layouts; they do not replace them.
- The final-result slot follows all result details/explanations. No ad is inserted
  among result numbers, warnings, cutting diagrams, textual alternatives or
  actions. The after-Calculate slot must preserve existing successful-result
  focus/scroll behavior so the requested answer remains immediately accessible.
- Calculator directory (`/calculators/`) has two inline slots on mobile and
  desktop: one after its introductory header and before the complete calculator
  list, and one after that list. In `src/pages/calculators/index.astro` they sit
  outside `.card-grid`, never among calculator cards or inside navigation links.
  Desktop rails supplement both. This instruction concerns the directory, not
  the individual calculator pages. Exact responsive creative sizes remain open.
- Each of the 11 individual calculator pages has three inline slots on mobile
  and desktop: after the introductory header immediately above `.calculator-shell`,
  immediately before "How this calculator works", and after that complete section.
  In `src/components/CalculatorPage.astro`, all three are outside the tool/form/
  result block and its live region. The last follows `.explanation-card`, before
  Keep planning. No ad splits the explanation's
  assumptions, methodology, worked example, limitations or FAQ. Desktop rails
  remain additional. This supersedes the initial single `calculator-end` slot.
- Main Guides directory (`/guides/`) has a responsive inline slot after each
  complete section: Start Here, Common Workflows and Quilting Reference, three
  slots total. In `src/pages/guides/index.astro`, each follows its entire
  `<section>`, outside the guide-card grid. They apply on mobile and desktop;
  desktop rails remain additional. This does not change individual guide articles.
- Quick Start (`/guides/getting-started/`) has two inline slots: immediately
  before `#before-you-start` ("Before you start"), and immediately before the
  complete "You completed the Quick Start" section. This is an explicit
  override of the general article rule, not two additional slots.
- Other guide articles, including Common Workflows and Quilting Reference,
  have one inline slot before the first substantive step/section and one after
  the last. Short articles (up to ten steps) therefore have two inline slots.
  Longer articles additionally have a slot at each ten-step block boundary:
  before steps 11, 21, 31 and so on, only when those steps exist. Introductory
  callouts, contents navigation, nested subheadings and shared footer/CTA are
  not extra steps. Nonnumbered articles use their ordered substantive sections.
  Never split a heading from its explanation, a table, example or instruction.
- The cadence is expressed as unique boundaries after 0, 10, 20, ... completed
  steps plus the final boundary after N steps. Deduplicate coincident boundaries;
  never add both a ten-step boundary unit and an end unit in the same gap.
  The current 30-step Full Project Planner Tutorial has four slots: before
  step1 (`#before-you-begin`), step11 (`#quantity`), step21 (`#fresh-fabric-plan`)
  and after the complete step30 (`#special-cases`). The contents block remains
  before the first slot. Enter a Quilt Cut List has two slots: before "Prepare
  one row per repeated cut", and after "Use More options only when needed".
  These article slots apply on mobile and desktop with additional desktop rails;
  they supersede the initial sole `guide-end` recommendation. Use explicit
  authored section boundaries during implementation, not runtime heading-count
  inference or counting the shared GuidePage navigation/CTA as article steps.
- Ad creative sizes are responsive to actual slot/container space, with stable
  reserved geometry. Rail and inline formats are selected separately. There is
  one mounted instance per logical slot, not duplicate responsive trees. No UA,
  touch or device-name detection. No rails where margins cannot accommodate them;
  mobile does not automatically inherit the desktop rail stack.
- Print/PDF stay ad-free. No overlays, automatic refresh or sticky behavior are
  added by this placement instruction. Controls, labels, entry flow and static
  content remain usable; project-data privacy remains mandatory.

This locks placement scope, not provider adoption, final creative dimensions,
unit count/spacing, consent configuration, account submission or live rollout.
Provider eligibility for individual routes, including error/noindex surfaces,
must be checked before serving; default template support is not a promise every
route can serve ads. The owner selected AdSense for readiness preparation on
2026-10-07; the verification-only meta tag is live in commit 7434e05. Review is
pending and no ad loader or production slots serve ads. Provider selection does
not approve consent configuration or rollout.

## Rationale

Owner refinement, 2026-10-07: halve side-rail units per page length. The local
mockup doubles the spacing between unit tops on each side: rectangle spacing
610px to1220px, skyscraper spacing850px to1700px. This keeps the first unit and
approximately halves each longer stack, rounding an odd count upward. Inline
placements remain as locked. Exact provider-supported formats remain pending.

The owner wants to use desktop margins along long pages and preserve inline
monetization opportunities on mobile. A section boundary can carry a clearly
separated banner without necessarily interrupting entry. The initial one-unit
cap, rails replacing inline, and blanket rejection of form-section banners were
assistant recommendations, not governing requirements. They must not override
the explicit owner instruction.

V1.1 content-spec section14 still governs outcomes: uninterrupted data entry,
clear controls, complete result explanations, no significant layout movement,
no ads in print and preserved tool trust. The owner-defined placements must be
designed and verified against these outcomes rather than rejected merely because
they occur within a form. No revenue/abandonment claim is established by current
sparse search data.

  if selected/configured. No site-applied zoom/stretch or custom creative sizing.
  Keep reserved selected heights, separate labels/spacing and ad-free print/PDF.

This locks the sizing design and existing placement/density decisions. The
isolated mockup subsequently passed its Chrome/Edge geometry, focus and print
checks on 2026-10-07. This does not establish provider acceptance or approve
production integration or rollout. Resolve provider review, project-data
compatibility, privacy/contact and consent before integration.

## Consequences

The active design/runbook is `../launch/postlaunch-ads-review.md`; its earlier
placement recommendations are historical and superseded where inconsistent.
Next design work must show the rail stacks and all four planner inline slots at
representative widths, verifying their actual bounds, reading/focus order,
control separation, result focus/scroll, reserved space and empty/failure states.
Finalized dimensions and narrow fallbacks are recorded above and in the ads
review. Google's [responsive tag guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9183460?hl=en)
describes supported AdSense sizing; real provider fill remains unverified.

Provider/privacy/contact/consent prerequisites, isolated script behavior,
project-data protection, performance, browser/print gates, kill switch and staged
owner-authorized rollout remain required. The live verification meta tag does not
enable ad serving.
