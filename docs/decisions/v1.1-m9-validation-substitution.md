# V1.1 M9 Validation Substitution

## Status

Accepted and complete on 2026-08-31.

## Decision

Eligible target quilters are not available for the planned five-user usability
study. At the user's explicit direction, M9 uses the completed product redesign
and exhaustive guided operator validation as the practical pre-M10 substitute
instead of remaining indefinitely blocked on unavailable recruitment.

M9 is complete. U01-U05 are not required before M10 and must not be restored to
the active task list unless the user explicitly changes this decision.

## Evidence

- The complete automated regression checkpoint passes with 196 tests across 18
  files, G01-G45 identifier coverage, deterministic/property coverage, typecheck,
  lint, scoped formatting, zero high-severity dependency vulnerabilities, and a
  32-page static build.
- Installed Chrome and Edge smoke passes all 29 indexable routes, planner and
  calculator workflows, persistence, privacy, accessibility, responsive layout,
  performance fallback, diagrams, text alternatives, and print/PDF checks.
- The guided operator suite completed A01-A02, B01-B04, C01-C04, D01-D05,
  E01-E03, F01-F03, G01-G02, H, I01-I03, J01-J03, and K with no open P0-P3
  issue.
- Human review covered desktop and responsive presentation, keyboard and 200%
  zoom, theme, copy, print preview, and concept wording. Deterministic oracles
  covered stock, purchase shortfall, pattern comparison, and allocation output.
- Every P2 issue exposed during the guided run was corrected at its shared
  boundary and retested before the suite passed.

## Limitation

This substitution does not prove unassisted comprehension by five external
target quilters. No U01-U05 session occurred, and project or launch claims must
not imply otherwise. Future real-user evidence may still motivate improvement,
but absent recruitment is no longer a pre-M10 blocker.

## Rationale

The original five-user study was a pragmatic usability gate, not a domain
correctness rule or statistical market study. With its participants unavailable,
the user chose a bounded evidence-based substitute: redesign the workflow in
response to observed friction, exercise it exhaustively through deterministic and
human-judgment checks, preserve the evidence limitation, and continue to the
separate competitive gate.

## Consequences

- M9 is recorded complete with an explicit divergence review.
- M10 becomes the active project gate.
- The unused target-user protocol remains as an optional future study artifact,
  not an active launch dependency.
- Any later evidence of repeated concept confusion must still be diagnosed at the
  shared boundary and corrected coherently.
