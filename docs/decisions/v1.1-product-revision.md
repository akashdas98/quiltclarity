# V1.1 Product Revision

## Decision

Adopt `docs/v1.1/` as the governing product, domain, UX, content, analytics, launch-gate, and implementation package. The completed V1 implementation remains the migration baseline and reusable infrastructure, but its planner-centered launch thesis is superseded.

V1.1 work follows milestones M0-M10 in `docs/v1.1/07_TECHNICAL_MIGRATION_PLAN.md`. Public launch remains blocked until the V1.1 correctness, usability, and current same-job competitive gates pass.

## Rationale

Competitive revalidation found that mixed-piece optimization and cutting diagrams alone are not a durable reason to choose the product. V1.1 focuses the product on the compound job of compiling external cut requirements, allocating exact project-local stock, calculating only the purchase shortfall, comparing pattern and fresh-fabric amounts independently, and producing one executable plan.

This revision preserves the static Astro architecture, deterministic TypeScript domain code, existing corrected calculator rules, local-only persistence, and placement-derived diagrams rather than starting a ground-up rewrite.

## Consequences

- `docs/v1.1/00_GOVERNING_AGENDA.md` governs strategy and scope.
- `docs/v1.1/02_GOLDEN_RULES_AND_TESTS.md` governs domain behavior and fixtures G01-G45.
- The remaining V1.1 documents govern functional acceptance, UX, content/SEO, analytics, competitive launch acceptance, and implementation order according to `docs/v1.1/README.md`.
- Older `docs/product/quilt_FINAL_*` documents remain historical implementation-baseline evidence and must not override V1.1.
- Existing behavior is preserved where V1.1 explicitly carries it forward; conflicts are recorded and remediated milestone-by-milestone.
- No backend, account, AI, permanent stash system, full quilt designer, or arbitrary-shape optimizer is introduced.
