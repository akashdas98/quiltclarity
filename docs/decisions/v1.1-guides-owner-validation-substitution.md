# V1.1 Guides Owner-Validation Substitution

## Status

Accepted by product authority on 2026-09-01 for the post-M10 Guides/help
overhaul.

## Decision

Replace the Guides bundle's requirement for recruited novice/target-quilter
sessions with one exhaustive, guided owner-operated validation suite.

The owner will execute the from-zero learning and result-comprehension tasks.
Codex will provide exact prompts and fixtures, capture observable outcomes,
inspect browser evidence, and distinguish unassisted completion from assisted
completion. Automated checks may prepare and corroborate the run but cannot
stand in for the owner's observations.

The replacement cases are:

- `MT-U01` Owner from-zero self-teaching: the owner starts at `/guides/`, uses
  only the shipped learning/help system, and completes the representative plan.
- `MT-U02` Owner result comprehension: without in-task coaching, the owner
  identifies Pattern says, Fresh-fabric plan, Buy now, and the cutting plan.

Both cases use `OWNER REQUIRED` before execution and `PASS`, `FAIL`, or
`ASSISTED` afterward. A PASS closes this checkpoint's usability requirement.

## Evidence rules

- Record the exact build/commit, viewport/browser, prompt, observed path,
  confusion points, assistance, and final answers.
- Any procedural or conceptual hint during a case changes its status to
  `ASSISTED`; fix the product and rerun from a clean state before PASS.
- Preserve screenshots or equivalent browser evidence for important states.
- Do not claim independent novice, recruited-quilter, population-level, or
  external comprehension evidence.

## Rationale

The owner explicitly requested a validation design executable by one person so
the project is not blocked on recruiting quilters. This follows the established
M9 substitution precedent while retaining an honest limitation and a stronger
reproducible operator protocol than simulated claims.

## Consequences

- External target-user testing is no longer a completion or launch blocker for
  this Guides checkpoint.
- Owner execution remains mandatory; Codex-only automation cannot mark MT-U01 or
  MT-U02 PASS.
- Post-launch feedback may still reveal comprehension issues not exposed by the
  owner-operated run.
- Completed M9 and M10 remain closed and are not reopened.
