# V1.1 Reconciled Execution and Result Decision

## Status

Accepted in V1.1 M6 on 2026-08-21.

## Context

M2 and M3 already produced deterministic selected placements across exact finite-stock bins and an optional purchased bolt, but the planner still rendered the older fresh-bolt-only result. Adding stock inputs without switching the complete result contract would allow shopping quantities and diagrams to describe different layouts. Re-running allocation in presentation would create the same contradiction through a second layout engine.

## Rationale

One selected reconciliation must remain the source of truth across purchase advice and cutting execution. A thin read-only adapter lets stock and purchased bins reuse the mature accessible SVG/text/print projector while preserving the domain boundary: the optimizer owns geometry, presentation owns only scaling and explanation. Keeping pattern comparison on the independent fresh scenario also prevents on-hand stock from making an external pattern allowance appear artificially excessive.

## Decision

- The planner calls `reconcileProject` as its authoritative calculation boundary. The older `planProject` remains a fresh-bolt compatibility/regression contract and is not combined with stock-aware output.
- Fabric cards own project-local stock entry through immediately editable presets or custom rectangles. Physical width, length, and quantity are authoritative; preset names are convenience metadata only.
- Optional pattern amount and assumed usable WOF remain a separate fresh-fabric comparison. On-hand stock and buy-now shortfall never change that basis.
- `src/lib/presentation/reconciled-project.ts` adapts the selected material-bin placements into the existing cutting-diagram and instruction contracts. It may shape compatibility metadata, but it cannot normalize, optimize, select, or move geometry.
- Purchased-bolt rows use existing strip-derived instructions. Finite-stock bins use exact placement/piece instructions where a strip sequence would be untruthful. Both retain fabric, piece-instance, and material-bin identity.
- Domain leftover rectangles are the only stock-leftover geometry shown. Safety and upward purchase rounding apply only to new fabric; exact stock coverage recommends zero purchase.
- Results follow the V1.1 hierarchy: project status, shopping list, per-fabric decision, existing-stock allocation, purchased plan, execution diagram/text, leftovers, assumptions/warnings, and methodology.
- Editing after a successful calculation preserves the prior visible plan but labels it stale and changes the action to “Recalculate”. Invalid submission blocks replacement and links each field error to its source control.

## Consequences

- Shopping output, instructions, SVG, accessible text, and leftover summaries all describe one selected reconciliation.
- Stock diagrams can share the established accessible/print-safe SVG machinery without adding an allocator.
- Print output is longer than the previous fresh-bolt fixture because the required stock/purchase decision hierarchy is printable; the browser gate locks deterministic pagination and diagram sizing in Chrome and Edge.
- M7 can add calculator routes and content without reopening planner allocation or result geometry.
