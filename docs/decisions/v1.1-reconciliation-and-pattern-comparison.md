# V1.1 Reconciliation and Pattern Comparison

## Decision

Implement purchase-shortfall reconciliation and pattern comparison as independent domain paths.

`src/lib/domain/reconciliation.ts` evaluates every validated M2 finite-stock candidate. For each candidate it sends the exact unmet instances through the existing fresh-bolt optimizer, translates purchased placements back to original instance identities, then selects lexicographically by raw additional purchase before cutting complexity, stock fragmentation/useful leftovers, purchased-bolt waste, and stable strategy order. Safety and purchase rounding apply only to the selected raw purchased length.

`src/lib/domain/pattern-comparison.ts` exposes `freshFabricScenario`, whose signature accepts fabric assumptions and normalized requirements but no stock. Pattern comparison reports pattern stated, fresh raw, fresh buffered, fresh recommended, and the signed difference `pattern stated - fresh recommended`. Differences are informational. A known pattern WOF unequal to current usable WOF emits a not-like-for-like warning.

`reconcileProject` is the integrated V1.1 domain result. It keeps fabrics independent and returns fresh planning, optional pattern comparison, stock/purchase allocations, shopping amounts, and aggregate coverage status. The existing `planProject` remains the UI-facing fresh-bolt contract until the scheduled result/presentation redesign can display stock and purchased bins from the same placement result.

## Rationale

Stock shortfall answers “what should I buy now?” Pattern comparison answers “how does the external reference compare with a fresh plan?” Sharing a stock-reduced basis would make fully covered projects report a false zero planning requirement and could imply the pattern is wrong.

Evaluating all bounded M2 assignments is necessary because the assignment that places the most pieces or leaves the prettiest remnant can still require a longer purchased bolt. Raw additional purchase therefore precedes every secondary quality.

Keeping the current planner controller on `planProject` prevents a transitional UI from showing stock-aware shopping alongside a fresh-bolt-only diagram. Later milestones can switch to `reconcileProject` when bin-aware projections and result hierarchy are implemented together.

## Consequences

- G32-G33 purchase shortfall and safety behavior use actual purchased-bolt geometry, not area subtraction.
- G37-G38 pattern comparison remains identical when stock changes and explicitly warns on WOF mismatch.
- Every purchase-enabled successful reconciliation accounts for each original piece instance exactly once across stock and purchase bins.
- Purchase-disabled results may be incomplete, but expose exact unmet instances and `null` purchase fields rather than fabricated material.
- No persistence schema, UI, presentation, dependency, service, or fresh-bolt optimizer behavior changes in M3.
