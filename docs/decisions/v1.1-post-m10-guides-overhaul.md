overhaul. The user will supply the governing handoff-document bundle when that
work is ready to begin.

Do not infer requirements, inspect unrelated archives, or start implementation
before the bundle is supplied and reviewed. Once its authority, scope,
acceptance criteria, and validation requirements are reconciled with the active
V1.1 contracts, implement and validate the overhaul as a separate coherent

continuation documents against the completed V1.1 product and guides overhaul.

project before resuming trademark, domain/brand, or launch-activation work.

## Rationale

The user has identified a dedicated guide-quality revision that must happen
after the product and competitive gates but before public identity and
deployment are finalized. Waiting for the promised bundle prevents speculative
scope, while recording the checkpoint now preserves the intended sequence
across session resets.

- The guides overhaul becomes a distinct pre-launch validation checkpoint.
- Continuation-document reconciliation becomes a distinct checkpoint after the

  is supplied and reconciled.
- The existing domain candidate, trademark/registrar, purchase, replacement,
  hosting, and public-origin workflow remains preserved provisionally; its
